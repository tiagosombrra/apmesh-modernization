# ADR-0029: own temporary area meshes and their sample publications

Status: accepted for the bounded resource-lifetime repair; qualification and
remaining scope are recorded in [the area audit](../evidence/STAGE3_TRIANGLE_AREA.md).

The scalar-returning area caller loses its raw MeshAdaptive and leaves patch and
sample publications tied to temporary work. Give MeshAdaptive automatic lifetime,
retain each factory unique owner until consuming adoption, and reuse the count
caller's selective nonallocating scope after checking identical publication rules.
Restore the preceding patch and reclaim fresh samples before physical graph
destruction. Keep preexisting samples/parameter/adjacency list nodes borrowed.

Preserve evaluation before the degree cap, build before replacement, selected-
degree reconstruction, first-triangle area, strict/inclusive error thresholds and
the historical unchanged minor_error_. Selected-zero rejection and partial timer/
curvature/selection writes remain observable. No numerical policy change, callback
abstraction, retry, shared ownership or full metadata rollback is introduced.

Controlled orchestration and actual-error integration use separate test targets.
Require paired original traces, independent analytic area checks, every observed
allocation denial, borrowed identity and real negative controls; retain all original
failures and unsupported/unmeasured paths. This is not scientific authorization.
