## Native comparison

The isolated MSVC Debug C++17 build completes in 7.01 s. One native
application completes in 39.45 s within 180 s. The guarded Step 2
predecessor, five ordinary prefix OBJs, physical residual and curve
requests, secondary split, tree edit requests and capped-request
ledger match byte for byte. All 23 contour samples match in order,
identity and coordinates. The cap remains 4.

Omit one original parent split in the replayed copy. Parent 24 at
level 3 replaces leaf IDs 26 to 29. The other 129 final leaf records
retain their IDs, depths and boxes. The native tree has 130 balanced
leaves instead of 133. The side-neighbor balance verifies directly
over all boxes. Template construction uses the same catalog and
area centroid. The selected block emits 11 QUADs instead of 16.
Its four contour-facing edges of about 0.12500 in model units
become two of about 0.25000. Two unchanged neighboring leaves
change template patterns due to a different neighbor scale. They
emit 12 to 11 and 16 to 11 seed QUADs respectively. The full seed
changes from 550 to 539 QUADs. Independent replay checks all 97
new front commits and the area centroids. The original and guarded
position sequences each complete five valid passes, with fixed
contour, native analytic lift and positive corner checks.
