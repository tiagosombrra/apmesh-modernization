**Scientific separation:** exact existence of an analytic bicubic representation for author-constructed controls is NOT a proof that an arbitrary STEP face admits the same representation. Curved R3 geometry is a synthetic deformation of a contact pattern, not a representation of the source cuboids. Sampling evidence does not substitute for certified distance bounds on arbitrary curved/trimmed CAD.

**Admission thresholds:** external G1/G2 (source rights/topology), R3 geometric feasibility, R4 conversion completeness, R5 accepted runtime generation and future method-specific QUAD/anisotropy qualification are separate review gates. ### R3.C2 evidence revision (2026-11-15)

The source CAD assembly was recreated from the pinned T/Cross source recipes. Native OCCT Common/Cut extracts a full smaller-arm face from a partially covered larger host face, producing two rectangular wings and an interface sheet. The two adjacent arm side faces complete a **five-sheet local junction per contact**; this is not the complete solid boundary. Real TopoDS_IsSame edge groups carry 3 face uses (2 T, 4 Cross), and indexed BP curve groups preserve the exact global control vertex IDs. Local sampling: 245/490 Bézier samples with maximum discrepancy 3.552713678800501e-15; 125/250 native BRep samples, maxima 1.3322676295501878e-15 / 0.0. The **19 new tests** and the earlier **44 tests** all pass locally, not the APMesh production regressions. A near-gap 1e-9 was initially falsely admitted by a fuzzy native Boolean; an *independent stricter* 1e-12 source-contact distance test now rejects gaps 1e-10 and larger (bounded tolerance test). See [native contact proof](R3_PARTIAL_CONTACT_PILOT.md) and [negative case matrix](R3C2_CASE_MATRIX.md). No original STEP asset, rights review, accepted TRI mesh or G1/G2 admission is asserted.
